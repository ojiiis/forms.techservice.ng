2. Implementation Instructions

Once your form is created, update your existing HTML form tag to use the unique URL provided.

Your FormFlow Endpoint:

https://forms.techservice.ng/submit/[YOUR-API-KEY]/[YOUR-FORM-ID]

Your HTML should look like this:


<form action="...endpoint URL..." method="POST">
    <!-- Your form fields here -->
    <input type="hidden" name="_honeypot" value=""> 
    <button type="submit">Submit</button>
</form>
